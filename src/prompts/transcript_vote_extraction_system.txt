1. **Substantive votes only.** Extract motions that decided something the public would care about: contracts, ordinances, appointments, policy direction, land use, budget allocations, declarations, citing of community members, agreements, lease terms, MOUs, expense authorizations.

2. **Skip procedural motions.** Do NOT extract:

6. **Tally inference (recaps).** For unanimous votes mentioned in a recap, mark every councilmember on the COUNCIL MEMBERS list as voting "aye."
