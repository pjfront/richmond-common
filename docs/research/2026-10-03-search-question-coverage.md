## Source-checked Point Molate case

The [March 2, 2010 official minutes, page 9](https://www.ci.richmond.ca.us/ArchiveCenter/ViewFile/Item/2809#page=9) describe an original motion and a substitute proposal. They record approval of the original with four ayes (Bates, Butt, Lopez, Rogers), one nay (McLaughlin), and two abstentions (Ritterman, Viramontes), with none absent. The passage does not record a separate roll call on the substitute proposal. That absence does not establish that it failed by a particular vote.

`stage-vote-source-review.ts` holds this exact J-2 item because its indexed motion/vote records conflict with the minutes. This review confirmed the source passage; it did not repair the database or independently verify every Point Molate result. The search and item page must retain the hold and original document until a source-checked correction is complete.

## Read-only contracts and bounds

- Agenda evidence: `web/src/lib/commons-search.ts`, `queries/commons-search.ts`, and `queries/search.ts`. The staged query uses the existing read-only `search_site` RPC, not `search_hybrid`, embeddings, or a generated answer. Keyword scope is at most 100 ranked agenda hits, plus 100 vote-explanation hits in votes mode; dates/tags narrow that retrieved set. Empty-keyword browse applies filters directly, with at most 2,000 accessible items.
- Votes: `vote-records.ts` normalizes per-motion choices, prioritizes official IDs over names, and preserves conflicts as `not-recorded`. Motion and vote sources remain visible; recording-derived records are tentative. Existing automated extraction is not independent source verification.
- Money: `queries/finance-public.ts` reads only `finance_public_events` and `finance_public_coverage` in scope `0660620:calendar-2026`, January 1–November 3, 2026, bounded to 5,000 events. Fifteen-minute cache freshness does not establish acquisition coverage; use each coverage row's `status`, `checked_at`, `activity_from`, `activity_through` and `limitations`.
- Provenance: finance rows retain `event_key`, signed `amount`/`amount_kind`, all three party identities, `filing_ids`, original `source_url`/`source_urls`, `extracted_at`, `source_tier` and `reconciliation_status`. `finance-ledger.ts` shares browse/export filters and separates receipt/transfer, loan, noncash, refund and independent-spending labels. A negative adjustment alone does not establish a cash refund.
- Local fixtures: `commons-search.test.ts`, `queries/commons-search.test.ts`, `queries/finance-public.test.ts`, and `finance-ledger.test.ts`. This document reviews their assertions; it does not replace running the final changed suite or hosted validation.
