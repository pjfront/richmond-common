2. June summary page 3's side panel prints $21,605 contributions/$15,605 expenditures under Jan–June and $73,300/$58,071 under July-to-date despite a June 30 cutoff. Keep the valid latest-period figures separate from that inconsistency.
3. January–April summary line 6/11 says $14,835 payments/expenditures, while line 15 and Schedule E say $15,605. Schedule E's attachment reports $15,605.11. Cash arithmetic follows $15,605. Do not silently choose one for a supposedly reconciled 2026 spending total.

5. April–May donor attachments contain dates printed **2025**, and two rows dated May 17 after the declared May 16 cutoff. January–April attachments also contain some dates printed 2025. Preserve raw dates and seek clarification; do not automatically rewrite years. The June attachment includes May 24–28 rows before its declared start.
6. The May 17–28 PDF has only **five pages**: Schedule A and E refer to attached spreadsheets that are absent from that downloaded PDF.
7. January–April IDs 216695016 and 217027183 are different scans/redactions of matching financial pages, both stamped April 22. Both amendment boxes are unchecked and both API amendment links null. Count the declared period once and retain both sources; a larger filing ID is not amendment authority.

These conflicts make the safe public treatment an explicit **reported-period subtotal**, a latest-period/cash snapshot, source-linked donor rows, and a compact explanation of the unresolved differences. Do not manufacture “unitemized” donor entries to fill extraction gaps.
